CoastPlay PRO – Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

CoastPlay PRO is provided by its developer, the seller named on its App Store page. Contact: our support page.

The short version. We do not collect your personal information. The app has no account with us, no ads and no tracking, and we run no servers that your gameplay passes through.

What stays on your device

  • Your console pairing. A pairing key that lets the app connect to your console, kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain). Never sent to us.
  • Your PlayStation sign-in. You sign in on Sony's own web page; the app never sees your password. It keeps the sign-in token Sony returns, in secure storage, so it can find your console when you are away from home.
  • Settings and setup progress.
  • A connection log. A short, plain-language record of recent connection attempts to help you see what failed. It contains no passwords, keys, picture or sound. "Record play quality" adds picture-rate and delay readings; it is off unless you turn it on.
  • Your home's internet address. Remembered so the app can reach your console when you are away.
  • Creator Mode (optional). Recordings and saved clips are stored only on your device. If you choose to send a live picture to your own computer, it goes straight to the address you type, not through us. A password you enter to control OBS on your computer is kept in secure storage.

You can delete all of this at any time: Settings → Remove all consoles and pairings, Settings → Sign out, Connection log → Clear, or delete the app.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

  • Picture, sound and controller input travel directly between your console and your device. They do not pass through any server of ours.
  • Sony. Signing in, and finding your console when away from home, contact Sony's PlayStation Network using your own account. Sony's privacy policy applies to that.
  • One address lookup. While you are on your home network the app makes one request to a public "what is my IP address" service (api.ipify.org) to learn your home's internet address. That service sees the address the request comes from, as any website would. Nothing about you or your console is sent with it.
  • Connection helpers when away from home. If the app has to use the PlayStation Network to find a path to your console, the connection software may also ask public address-lookup ("STUN") servers what your device's internet address looks like from outside, and may ask your own router to open a port if it supports that. Those services see your internet address, as any website would. No account details, console details or gameplay are sent to them.
  • Support reports. Only if you choose "Create a support report" and send it yourself. It contains your device model, system and app version, and the connection log.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no sale or sharing of personal data, and no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
