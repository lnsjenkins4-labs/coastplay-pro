CoastPlay PRO

Support

CoastPlay PRO shows the picture and sound from your own PlayStation 5 on your iPhone and sends your controller back to it. The game keeps running on your console.

Get help
Open an issue on our public page and describe what happened: github.com/lnsjenkins4-labs/coastplay-pro/issues.
Please do not post passwords, pairing codes or other private details.

Play your Mac

Download CoastPlay Host for Mac (free, macOS 14 or later): CoastPlay-Host.zip
Unzip it, drag it to Applications and open it. Its window has a short setup list. The Mac has to be on, with the Host open, whenever you play it from your phone, at home or away.

Before you ask

  • What has to be on: at home, your PS5 switched on. Away from home, your PS5 switched on at home and one setting on your router (the app's Help tab has a guide).
  • Same Wi-Fi first. Set up and test on the same Wi-Fi as the console. Away from home is new and does not work on every network.
  • Console settings. On the PS5: Settings → System → Remote Play → Enable Remote Play. In Power Saving → Features Available in Rest Mode, turn on Stay Connected to the Internet and Enable Turning On PS5 from Network.
  • Controller. Connect it to the iPhone by Bluetooth or cable, not to the console. Or use the on-screen controls.
  • Send us a report. In the app: Help → Create a support report. You choose whether to send it; it holds your device model, versions and a short connection log, no passwords.
